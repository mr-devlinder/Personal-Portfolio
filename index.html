About Me

Hi — I'm Lucas Linder, a student from Clovis North High School in Fresno, CA with a passion for technology and a clear vision for my future — becoming a cybersecurity engineer and computer science professional.

My coding journey has given me hands-on experience with Python, where I have developed foundational programming skills including logic building, automation, and scripting. Alongside that, I have explored HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, building an understanding of how the web works from the ground up.

I am also actively learning Linux, diving into the command line, file system navigation, and system operations. Linux is a cornerstone of the cybersecurity world, and developing fluency in it early has given me a stronger foundation for understanding how operating systems function at a deeper level.

These experiences have sharpened my problem-solving mindset, my attention to detail, and my ability to think critically about how systems are built and where they can break.
