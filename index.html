About Me

Hi — I'm Lucas Linder, a high school student from Fresno, CA with a passion for technology and a clear vision for my future — becoming a cybersecurity engineer and computer science professional.

My coding journey has given me hands-on experience with Python, where I have developed foundational programming skills including logic building, automation, and scripting — all of which are essential tools in the cybersecurity field. Alongside that, I have explored HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, building an understanding of how the web works from the ground up, from structuring content to styling interfaces and adding interactivity.

I am also actively learning Linux, diving into the command line, file system navigation, and system operations. Linux is a cornerstone of the cybersecurity and computer science world, and developing fluency in it early has given me a stronger foundation for understanding how operating systems function at a deeper level — a critical skill for any aspiring security engineer.

These experiences have taught me more than just syntax — they have sharpened my problem-solving mindset, my attention to detail, and my ability to think critically about how systems are built and where they can break.

I am currently working toward a future in computer science and cybersecurity, with a strong interest in areas such as ethical hacking, network security, and secure software development. I am committed to continuously growing my technical skill set, seeking out new challenges, and building the knowledge base needed to protect the digital world.

I believe that great engineers are not just coders — they are curious thinkers who never stop learning. That is exactly the kind of professional I am working every day to become.
